# Approved proof points (anonymised)

These match Kareena's live "No sale, no fee" campaign (99 Degrees Tech). Edit as results change. Only use what's listed here. Never name or hint at specific clients in writing.

## The offer
- We bring back visitors who left, with ads across premium sites including Google's network, bought through our own ad platform and direct publisher partnerships.
- The brand only pays when someone clicks our ad and buys. **No sale, no fee.**
- We only count sales from a click on our ad (no view-through credit).
- No setup fee, no minimum spend. Setup is one site tag.

## Results
- "We normally see a ROAS of 5x or more." Use this wording only. If asked how it's measured: sales from a click on our ad, divided by what the brand paid us.
- Aggregated share-of-sales (use carefully, "in the best months" only): retargeting has driven roughly 2–9% of total site sales for AU apparel and footwear brands.

## Conversion stats by vertical (as used in outreach)
- General: fewer than 3 in 100 visitors buy.
- Fashion, footwear, accessories: fewer than 3 in 100.
- Home, furniture, bedding: about 1 in 100.
- Jewellery, luxury: fewer than 1 in 100.

## How to describe the client base
- "AU fashion, footwear and outdoor brands, and a large comparison site, at 80k+ visits a month."
- Never: brand names, logos, monthly revenue figures, or charts that include April–June 2026.

## Claims to avoid
- "Better than every other retargeting company" (unprovable). Instead: "happy to run alongside your current setup for a month."
- Any specific ROAS, revenue or conversion-rate promise for a particular prospect.
